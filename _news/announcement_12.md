**BreathYou Co., Ltd.** received the Korea Entrepreneurship Foundation Chairperson's Award (Encouragement Award) at SPRINT Next Unicorn's Day (November), following Excellence Awards at three Sungkyunkwan University startup programs in 2026: the Consortium Lab-based Startup Camp (August), the Lab Startup Competition (September), and the Pre-TeX-Corps Business Plan Presentation (October). The team also completed the Ministry of Education's 2026 Student Startup Promising Team 300+ program (Leap Track) in October, receiving a certificate from the Minister of Education.
