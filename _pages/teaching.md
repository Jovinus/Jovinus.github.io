## Courses

### Exercise and Digital Healthcare

University of Seoul · Department of Sport Science · Fall 2026

A one-credit practicum for fourth-year undergraduates on where digital healthcare and exercise
science meet: students map the industry, collect activity and heart-rate data from their own bodies,
and build a prediction formula from it.
