## Courses

**[Exercise and Digital Healthcare](/teaching/exercise-and-digital-healthcare/) — University of Seoul**
Fourth-year undergraduate elective, Department of Sport Science, Fall 2026. A one-credit practicum on
where digital healthcare and exercise science meet: students map the industry, collect activity and
heart-rate data from their own bodies, and build a prediction formula from it. [Full syllabus](/teaching/exercise-and-digital-healthcare/) · [PDF](/assets/pdf/exercise_and_digital_healthcare_syllabus_fall2026.pdf)
