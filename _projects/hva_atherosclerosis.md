**Period:** 2021.05 -- 2022.01

**Affiliation:** Samsung Medical Center

**Summary:**
Analyzed the impact of healthy vascular aging (HVA) and cardiorespiratory fitness on subclinical atherosclerosis using health screening center data.

**Key Responsibilities:**

- Literature review and inclusion & exclusion criteria design
- Built analysis database by querying and merging multiple data tables from health screening DB
- Large-scale clinical data cleaning and derived variable generation (Physical Activity, HVA from lab, questionnaire, and exercise stress test data)
- Logistic regression analysis of the relationship between CRF, HVA status, and subclinical atherosclerosis

**Outcome:** 1 SCI publication (featured in BRIC's _People Who Brightened Korea_ — a curation of Korean first/corresponding-author papers in journals with IF ≥ 10 and JCR top 10%)
